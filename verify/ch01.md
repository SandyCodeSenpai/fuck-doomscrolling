## Notes
- The book's durability math uses a factor of 2 (2 x 4.3e-5 x 4.3e-5) for the ordering of the second and third failures; cards reproduce it exactly as printed.
- Table cost figures mix years and sources (consumer NVMe late 2025 vs Backblaze HDD Nov 2022). Cards keep them as printed, so treat SSD vs HDD price ratios as rough.
- Several cards contain textbook reasoning not stated in the chapter (e.g. why AHCI's single queue suited HDDs, LSM trees turning small writes sequential, TLC for write-heavy data). I checked these and they are correct, so they stay.
