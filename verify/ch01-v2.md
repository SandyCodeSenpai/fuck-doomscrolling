## Fixes

- ch01-005 [UNSUPPORTED] The stat "1.67 EB" is derived by subtraction. It is not a number the chapter states. I replaced it with ">50%", "storage cost cut Microsoft said EC could bring", which is the chapter's "over 50%".
- ch01-005 [MISLEADING] deeper said Haystack's bytes "were stored 5.3 times because of the read rate, not because durability needed it". The ideal 3.6× was for durability. Only the rise above it was bought for IOPS. Fixed.
- ch01-017 [MISLEADING] Same Haystack overstatement in deeper. Fixed the same way.
- ch01-009 [MISLEADING] One mcq distractor was "Failure to beat DRAM latency". That is true: Optane DIMMs were about 10× slower than DRAM. A learner could argue for it. Replaced with "A security flaw in Optane DIMMs".
- ch01-025 [WRONG] deeper said SMR puts "more bits under the head per rotation, so sequential throughput grows". SMR gains capacity by overlapping tracks, which does not raise bits per rotation. Rewritten as a general density statement plus the book's throughput figures.
- ch01-029 [UNSUPPORTED] deeper said EBS and Persistent Disk are "backed by a distributed storage layer underneath". The chapter does not say this. Replaced with a statement the chapter supports: they are block examples, and so is Ceph RBD.
- ch01-031 [MISLEADING] deeper said object stores are "ruled out" as database backing. The book only says block is used for databases. Softened to "a poor direct backing".
- ch01-032 [MISLEADING] The mcq asked which interface is "the only one" with a hierarchical namespace, with "Object" as a distractor. ch01-033 teaches that Google Rapid Storage gives an object store a hierarchical namespace, so the distractor is arguably right. Rewrote the mcq as a "which namespace does each interface have (classic table)" match.
- ch01-033 [MISLEADING] body said Rapid Storage "breaks all three" usual object traits, including "whole-object replacement". The book gives it unlimited appends, not in-place edits. I restated that trait as "no way to add to an object without replacing it", which appends do break.
- ch01-035 [MISLEADING] deeper said availability is "hurt mostly by transient events". The book only says that fewer than 10% of node outages last over 15 minutes. Reworded. Also softened "so Standard stays reachable" (see Notes, One Zone-IA).
- ch01-036 [MISLEADING] deeper went from the Azure paper's title to "careful design can deliver both". Reworded as the paper's claim.
- ch01-038 [MISLEADING] deeper said "Google's delay came from measured outage durations". The book links the study to GFS's 15-minute wait but does not give the history of how the delay was chosen. Reworded as "the study backs the delay with measured durations".
- ch01-042 [MISLEADING] deeper said "The data's age, not its type, picks the scheme". The chapter maps schemes by data type (messages on replicated SSD, for example). Changed to "For the same data, age picks the scheme".
- ch01-044 [UNSUPPORTED] deeper said "Most failures are a single fragment". The chapter gives no such statistic. Reworded so the single lost fragment is "the common case" LRC optimises.
- ch01-045 [MISLEADING] The deeper lead-in was "Nothing important traded away". LRC does give up RS(12,4)'s guarantee of surviving any 4 failures (ch01-044). Changed to "Having it both ways".
- ch01-053 [UNSUPPORTED] deeper said "S3 Standard spreads data across at least 3 zones" and "only the multi-zone class is built to stay reachable through a zone outage". The chapter says only "most classes". Rewritten to use only the chapter's facts, with One Zone-IA hedged "as its name suggests". The mcq why claimed "stays reachable through a zone failure". Changed to "no single zone holds every copy".
- ch01-015, ch01-056 [MISLEADING] deeper said power per TB is "one reason tape keeps its place/role in archives". This causal link is inference. Softened to "a point in tape's favour for archives".
- ch01-057 [MISLEADING] deeper said services "can gain from [L4] without changing". verify/ch01.md had already removed this L4 overclaim, and the rewrite brought it back. Removed. The deeper now states what L4 does.
- ch01-060 [UNSUPPORTED] deeper gave "separate clusters each need their own headroom" as the cause of the 10× cluster cut. The chapter gives the number, not the cause. Removed. The card now uses only the tenant-mixing reason the chapter supports, plus a restatement of the HDFS-to-Tectonic move.
- ch01-062 [UNSUPPORTED] The hook said these four types "make up most of the bytes in a big app". The chapter never says this. New hook: "Photos, logs, analytics and backups look nothing alike. What do they have in common?"
- ch01-063 [WRONG] The mcq's correct option said EC's costs hit "small writes and repairs, which cold data rarely triggers". Cold data needs repair after disk failures just as often as hot data. Changed to "small writes and degraded reads", which matches the card's own why. The deeper "EC's weaknesses barely bite" was narrowed the same way.

## Notes: the 12 flagged facts from verify/ch01-style.md
1. **ch01-035/053/034/037, One Zone-IA is single-zone.** This is inferred from the class name, which is in the chapter. It is true in reality. I kept it, hedged "as its name suggests". I removed the stronger inferred claim that "only the multi-zone class stays reachable" (053) and the claim that "Standard stays reachable" (035).
2. **ch01-022, QLC for read-mostly and TLC for write-heavy.** Correct textbook reasoning that follows from the chapter's "QLC has lower endurance". Kept.
3. **ch01-061, LSM turns small writes sequential and suits flash.** Correct textbook reasoning. Kept.
4. **ch01-014, "tiny bits are hard to write stably".** A faithful paraphrase of "so smaller bits can be written stably". Kept.
5. **ch01-038, year 2010.** Supported. Check-your-understanding Q6 says "Google's 2010 study", and source [16] is OSDI 2010. Kept.
6. **ch01-036, Azure paper title.** The title is quoted exactly from the chapter's Sources list [15]. Kept, but reworded as the paper's claim (see Fixes).
7. **ch01-023, 0.99^100 ≈ 37%.** Checked: 0.366. So about 63% of page loads are slow, which is correct. This is general probability, consistent with the chapter's "likely". Kept.
8. **ch01-010/016, SSD vs HDD price ratio.** Both figures come from the chapter's table (late 2025 NVMe, Nov 2022 HDD), and the cards flag the ratio as rough. The ch01-007 quiz's "several times more" (3.6-7×) holds. Kept.
9. **ch01-060, "headroom" as the cause of the 10× cut.** Unsupported as a cause. Removed.
10. **ch01-056/015, power per TB and tape.** The link between the two is inference. Softened to "a point in its favour".
11. **ch01-047, the factor of 2.** Printed exactly as in the book, and it is mathematically right: after the first failure, either of 2 remaining disks can fail. 2 × (4.3e-5)² = 3.7e-9, and 0.047 × 3.7e-9 = 1.7e-10. Kept.
12. **ch01-064, road map.** The 13 topics match chapters 2-14. Its hook implied an ordering rationale the body did not give, so I rewrote the hook.
