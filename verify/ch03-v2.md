## Notes
- The book itself says "mean time to repair appears in the denominator of the loss probability" (section "How S3 reaches 11 nines"). That wording is imprecise: MTTR is in the denominator of MTTDL, and loss probability grows with MTTR. The card follows the correct meaning.
- ch03-035 says the date-prefix hot spot comes back every day because each new date is a fresh, unsplit range. The book does not state this, but it follows from range partitioning, so it was kept.
- Some deeper text adds textbook background the book does not state (GFS single master, Haystack metadata not fitting in cache, reasons for Azure's 70% target). These were left in because they are accurate background.
